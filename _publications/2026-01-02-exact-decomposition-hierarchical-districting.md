This work develops an exact method for hierarchical districting and facility location problems arising in election planning. The approach is a logic-based Benders decomposition whose NP-hard subproblem is accelerated by a spanning-tree dynamic programming oracle and a new family of problem-specific cuts, and it solves instances 100x larger than previous methods in the literature.
